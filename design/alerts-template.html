Your private alerts link

Treat this link like a password.

Anyone with it can use your alert permissions. Paste it only into your own OBS Browser Source. Do not share it or show it on stream.

The token is in the URL fragment, so website requests do not include it. Your browser sends it directly to Twitch to connect alerts.

Expiry & reconnect

Alerts work until Twitch access expires or is revoked. The editor shows the expiry returned by Twitch, not a fixed lifetime. Reconnect alerts, copy the new private link, then replace the URL in OBS.

If access was exposed, revoke it first. Deleting a Browser Source alone does not revoke Twitch access. Chat and sample tests keep working.
