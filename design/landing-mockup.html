A few things
before you go live.

Does it need my Twitch password?

No. Type your Twitch channel name to use public chat. Connecting with Twitch is optional; you sign in on Twitch, never in ChatSkins.

Will it slow my stream?

ChatSkins uses lightweight chat layers, not video backgrounds. Performance depends on your settings and computer. Start with a simple skin and turn off animation if needed.

Can I use it with Streamlabs or StreamElements?

Use the link in a Browser Source in Streamlabs Desktop or OBS. With a StreamElements setup, add ChatSkins as a separate OBS Browser Source alongside your existing overlay.

Will 7TV, BTTV and FFZ emotes show up?

Public global and channel emotes are supported. If an emote service is unavailable, the emote’s name appears as text so chat stays readable.

Do I need to be live to set this up?

No. Sample chat lets you design offline. To test the real overlay, send a message in your Twitch channel’s chat; your stream can stay offline.

How do I save my changes?

Your overlay link contains your settings. Keep it somewhere handy, or save a skin in this browser from the Advanced editor. After editing, copy the new link into OBS.
