A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you press go live.

Is it really free?

Yes. All 42 skins and ordinary chat overlay setup are free. No login is required.

Will it work with my streaming app?

If it supports a Browser Source or web source, you can add your overlay URL. The docs cover dimensions and source settings.

Can I change everything later?

Yes. Open the editor, adjust your look, then copy the new link into your Browser Source.

Does ChatSkins post in my chat?

Ordinary chat is read-only. It displays messages and does not send chat or moderate viewers.
