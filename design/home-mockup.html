Before you go live

Do I need to connect Twitch?

No. Your channel name is enough for public chat. Optional Twitch sign-in fills it in for you.

Can I set up while offline?

Yes. Design with sample chat, then send a message in your Twitch chat to test your OBS source. Your stream can stay offline.

Will it slow my stream?

Skins use lightweight chat layers, without video backgrounds. Performance depends on your settings and computer; turn down motion if needed.

How do I save my changes?

Keep your overlay link, or save a skin in Advanced. After editing, copy the new link into OBS.

What if an emote service is unavailable?

Its emote names appear as text so the conversation stays readable.

Can I ask for a new look?

Absolutely. Send the idea to the quest board.
