# Frames

Frame templates place illustrated artwork around the chat window. The chat stays inside the open center, and the art stays transparent outside its edge.

Pick a shape that fits your layout: **tall** for a side chat, **wide** for a bottom strip, or **square** for a box. Use the frame's shape choices in the editor when they are available.

## Set the plaque
The plaque shows your channel name by default. In **Style · Frame**, edit **Title text** and the **LIVE CHAT** sub-label. The title shrinks to fit the plaque.

## Match your style
In the Frame card, choose **Frame color**, or select **Match my brand color** to use your current brand color. Set a glow color and strength, then choose **Off**, **Pulse**, or **Shimmer**.

Set **Middle background** to **None**, **Color**, or **Image**, then adjust its opacity. This changes the area behind chat inside the frame. **Message borders** are off by default; turn them on to add outlines around message rows.

Some templates have animated accents such as sparkles, hearts, or drifting particles. Reduced-motion settings turn those accents and animated glow off.

## Add a matching webcam frame
Some frames include a **Matching webcam frame** link. Open it and add the page as a second Browser Source in OBS, alongside your chat source. It shows the matching frame around the camera area. Size and position that source over your webcam.
